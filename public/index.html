sigma-lab

Detection-as-code lab. Paste Sigma rules and labelled events, see precision and recall per rule, lint warnings and MITRE ATT&CK coverage against your own threat profile. Runs in this tab, nothing is uploaded.

Sigma rules (YAML, several separated by ---)

Events (JSON Lines, optional "_expect": ["rule-id"] labels)

Threat profile (ATT&CK technique IDs, optional)

Supported: field modifiers contains, startswith, endswith, all, re, windash, exists, lt/gt, wildcards, keyword lists, and/or/not, parentheses, "1 of" and "all of". Aggregations (count, near) are not supported and are reported.
